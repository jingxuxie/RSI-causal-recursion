# Critical positioning audit (15 September 2026)

The paper should not be pitched as inventing self-improvement evaluation, learning mutation policies, or robust concentration.

## Closest verified primary sources

- STOP (Zelikman et al., 2024; arXiv:2310.02304) evaluates an improver obtained through self-application. Testing learned improvers on downstream problems is not new.
- Huxley-Goedel Machine (Wang et al., 2025; arXiv:2510.21614) distinguishes descendant productivity from current task performance. That distinction alone is not a contribution here.
- Hyperagents (Zhang et al., 2026; arXiv:2603.19461) evaluates improvement potential, fixed meta-level controls, and transfer. The paper must not claim the first fixed-author baseline or transfer audit.
- Mossina, Rachelson, and Delahaye (2019; arXiv:1905.03726) study learned control of evolutionary mutation probabilities. A learned mutation rule is not itself a novel RSI mechanism.
- Nilim and El Ghaoui (2005), robust MDPs: rectangular robust Bellman recursion is established. Preserving persistent shared parameters rather than independently choosing rows is an application of that distinction.
- Maurer and Pontil (2009; arXiv:0907.3740) supply the empirical Bernstein inequality used here, including the rescaling and union-bound constants.
- Chen, Gao, and Ren (2016; arXiv:1511.04144) and Vallinayagam, Pensia, and Jog (2026; arXiv:2605.24741) establish nearby robust-decision and robust-testing foundations. The latter explicitly studies least-favorable distributions, uncertainty-set overlap, and contamination-sensitive sample complexity. Our endpoint separation boundary is not a new general theory of robust testing.

## Defensible current positioning

The proposed contribution is a comparator-specific causal audit framework that separates frozen-mechanism gain from the effect of recursively inheriting authorship, specifies when a transplant is meaningful, and quantifies or exposes ambiguity when it is not. The finite-state constructions make the assumptions executable. The independent conditional-tree result adds a concrete finding: positive learned-improver gains can coexist with a certified negative inheritance dividend. The all-permutation diagnostic retains an unhelpful result and does not overclaim a mechanism.

## Not established

A comprehensive novelty clearance across causal inference, hyper-heuristics, evolutionary self-adaptation, and algorithm configuration has not been independently completed. The statistical and dynamic-programming tools are largely standard. Whether their integrated application plus the two bounded representations is sufficient for a main-conference contribution remains a substantive review question, not something guaranteed by passing software tests.

## Venue packaging checked

The official ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines specify at most nine main-text pages at submission and a required AI-use statement outside that limit. The current main text uses eight pages and includes a truthful disclosure. The working paper is not submitted. Public repository ownership must not be exposed by links in anonymous supplementary material.
